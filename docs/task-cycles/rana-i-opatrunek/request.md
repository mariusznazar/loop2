## What you will see

**A new skill tree for dressing wounds.** The character screen has a ninth skill tree, „Opatrywanie małych ran”, last in the list after „Unik przed ciosem”. It grows by one point of practice each time the character finishes making a bandage („Wytwórz — Opatrunek”) or finishes dressing a wound with one („Opatrz — Opatrunek”), and gives a level and a point at 10, 28, 60 and 110 practice. Its branches, with the numbers of the earlier game:

- „Mocniejszy zwój”: a bandage heals 5 health more per rank (prices 1 · 1 · 2);
- „Wprawna dłoń”: „Opatrz” takes 2 minutes less per rank (prices 1 · 1 · 2, never under 1 minute);
- „Szybkie gojenie”: health comes back by itself 0,05 faster per game minute per rank (prices 2 · 3); the screen writes such values with a decimal comma and no computer rounding („0,05 → 0,1”, „0,15”);
- „Ucisk” (price 3): a branch that is not a number. It opens a new fight action, „Ucisk — Opatrunek”.

**„Ucisk” in a fight.** „Opatrz” still does not work in a fight; „Ucisk” is dressing a wound in one turn instead of ten minutes. The player writes it as a fight rule with a health threshold of their own, for example „health below 50 → Ucisk — Opatrunek”, before „Uderz — patyk”. In its turn it uses up one bandage from a belt pocket (an empty pocket takes one from the backpack in the same turn, and only an item that fits a pocket) and gives back as much health as „Opatrz” would with the same bandage — 25 today, more with „Mocniejszy zwój” — never above full health. At full health it does nothing and the rule is greyed „zdrowie pełne”. In the program editor it stands in the group „Żywność i walka” after „Odskocz”, with the rolled bandage icon of „Opatrz”, the time „tura”, no price at its cell and „(1 opatrunek)” at its target. Before the branch is bought it is greyed „gałąź niewykupiona”; outside a fight a rule with it is amber „tylko w walce”; with no bandage anywhere „brakuje 1 × opatrunek”. The paragraph of an encounter reads „Mag uciska ranę: zdrowie 71/100” (a whole number, as the body row shows it) and the chronicle adds „Bierze opatrunek z plecaka do kieszeni” when a pocket is refilled.
