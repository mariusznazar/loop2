### Slice 1 — the mechanism of a rung and the three rungs in the data

- **Goal:** `src/browser/sim/data/maps.ts` holds the mechanism of a rung: a rung stands as a row of its own that names its opener and gives only the numbers that differ from it (row 2), and the code of that file composes from the row and its opener a whole map of `MAPS` — the resources matched to the opener's by identifier and the attackers by identifier, the solving condition by kind, the region and everything else taken from the opener, the creatures never changed — so that `MAPS` holds each rung as it holds every map and `MapId` names it. A row that would add a resource, an attacker or a kind of solving condition the opener lacks cannot be written (a type error, or a refusal a test shows). The name of a rung is composed by that code (row 3): the name of the first map of its line of rungs that is no rung, with the Roman numeral of its place in the line from II; no data writes it. The three rungs `forestDeep2`, `forestDeep3` and `forestDeep4` stand in the data — „Las — głębiej II”, „Las — głębiej III” and „Las — głębiej IV” — the first opened by `forestDeep`, each next by the one before it (row 1 of *Settlements to record*), in the order of `MAPS` that row 5 gives: `forestEdge`, `forest`, `forestDeep`, `forestPack`, `forestDeep2`, `forestDeep3`, `forestDeep4`, `forestLair`, written in that order by hand. Each rung row writes by hand its sentence of the solving condition with its own numbers, its short sentence of the demand under the card's heading with its own hours and attacks (D-327), and its completion text, which repeats the composed name („Las — głębiej II rozwiązany”). The rungs' numbers are provisional here and settled by Slice 6: a rung raises its streak and its beaten attacks, and the chance and cooldown of the wolf's attack where it changes them, and keeps the grid, the entrance, the temperature, the step, the resources and the caps of `forestDeep` (the plan's *Choices*).
