The Maps screen of the game now shows a catalog of maps, as the old Loop did, instead of a plain list. Maps stand in groups by region with a counter of solved maps ("Las · 1 z 5"; the counter counts every map of the region, also those you have not opened yet), above them a search field that finds open maps by the name of the map or of a resource, and three views of the same list: "Dokąd" (open maps on top, solved maps folded under one row), "Co daje" (maps grouped by the resource they give, with how many tiles of it and how often it returns) and "Postęp" (open maps, and an empty unnamed place for every map you have not opened).
